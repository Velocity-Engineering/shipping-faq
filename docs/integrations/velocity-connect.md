# Velocity Connect FAQ

Velocity Connect lets you surface Velocity's financing and growth products — such as working capital offers — to your customers and your business at key moments like checkout.

---

## Overview

### Q: What is Velocity Connect?
**A:** Velocity Connect is an integration layer that connects your Velocity Shipping account with Velocity's broader financial products. It enables:
- Displaying relevant financing or growth offers to merchants at appropriate touchpoints in the dashboard
- Linking your shipping activity with Velocity's lending and analytics products
- Streamlining onboarding to Velocity Finance products for existing Shipping customers

### Q: Who can use Velocity Connect?
**A:** Velocity Connect is available to merchants who have an active Velocity Shipping account. Eligibility for specific financial products (e.g. revenue-based financing) depends on your shipment history and business profile. Contact your KAM to check eligibility.

### Q: Do I need to do anything to enable Velocity Connect?
**A:** For most merchants, Velocity Connect works in the background once your account is linked. You may see offers or prompts in your dashboard when you are eligible for a product. For API-level integration, contact your KAM or email support.shipping@velocity.in.

---

## Data & Privacy

### Q: What data does Velocity Connect share between products?
**A:** Velocity Connect uses your shipping and business data (shipment volumes, delivery rates, revenue signals) to assess eligibility for financing products. This data stays within the Velocity platform and is not shared with third parties. See Velocity's privacy policy for full details.

### Q: Can I opt out of Velocity Connect?
**A:** If you do not wish to see financing offers or have your shipping data used for eligibility assessments, contact support.shipping@velocity.in to adjust your preferences.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Q: I'm not seeing any Velocity Connect offers in my dashboard.
**A:** Offers are only shown when you meet the eligibility criteria for a product. Common reasons you might not see an offer:
- Insufficient shipment history (typically 3+ months of activity required)
- Account is new or hasn't reached minimum volume thresholds
- Offer has already been acknowledged or dismissed

Contact your KAM if you believe you should be eligible but aren't seeing any offers.
